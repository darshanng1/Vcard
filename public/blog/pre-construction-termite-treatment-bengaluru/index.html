Pre Construction Termite Treatment in Bengaluru

Pre construction termite treatment in Bengaluru is essential to protect your new building from future termite damage. Due to soil conditions and moisture levels in Bengaluru, subterranean termite infestation is common.

What is Pre Construction Anti Termite Treatment?

It is a preventive soil treatment performed before laying the foundation and flooring. A chemical barrier is created beneath the structure to prevent termites from entering from the ground.

Why is Termite Treatment Necessary Before Construction?

  • Protects foundation from underground termites
  • Prevents future damage to wooden doors and interiors
  • More economical than post construction treatment
  • Avoids drilling and damage after flooring is completed

Stages of Pre Construction Termite Treatment

  1. Bottom soil treatment before PCC
  2. Foundation trench treatment
  3. Plinth filling soil treatment
  4. Treatment around pipelines and joints
  5. External perimeter treatment

Who Should Take This Service in Bengaluru?

Individual house owners, apartment builders, commercial developers, warehouse owners and factory projects in Bengaluru planning new construction.

Chemicals Used

We use IS standard compliant and government approved anti termite chemicals such as Imidacloprid and Chlorpyrifos formulations.
