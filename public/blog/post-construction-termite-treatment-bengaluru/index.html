Post Construction Termite Treatment in Bengaluru

Post construction termite treatment in Bengaluru is essential when termites are already present inside an existing building. If you notice hollow wooden doors, mud tubes on walls, or damaged furniture, immediate professional treatment is required.

What is Post Construction Termite Treatment?

It is a corrective termite control process performed after construction is completed. The treatment involves drilling small holes along walls and injecting anti-termite chemicals into the soil beneath the structure.

Signs You Need Termite Treatment

  • Hollow sound in wooden doors or frames
  • Powder falling from furniture
  • Mud tubes along walls
  • Swollen door frames
  • Visible termite activity

How We Perform Termite Treatment

  1. Detailed inspection of affected areas
  2. Drilling at regular intervals along walls
  3. Injection of approved anti-termite chemicals
  4. Sealing drilled holes neatly
  5. Perimeter soil treatment if required

Why Professional Treatment is Important

Termites spread silently inside walls and flooring. DIY sprays do not eliminate the root colony. Professional chemical barrier treatment ensures long-term protection.
