Bird Netting in Bangalore – Balcony & Pigeon Safety Net Guide (2026)

Bird netting in Bangalore is one of the most effective long-term solutions to protect residential balconies and apartments from recurring pigeon infestation. With rapid vertical development across areas like Hebbal, Whitefield, Yelahanka, HSR Layout, and Electronic City, pigeon nesting has become a major concern for homeowners and apartment associations.

Pigeon droppings are acidic and can damage railings, corrode AC outdoor units, stain balcony tiles, and create serious hygiene risks. Regular cleaning only removes visible waste but does not prevent re-nesting.

Why Bird Netting Is Necessary in Bangalore

Bangalore’s climate supports year-round breeding. Open balconies provide ideal nesting spots. Without a physical barrier, pigeons will return repeatedly.

  • Prevents pigeon nesting permanently
  • Protects hygiene and air quality
  • Prevents structural damage
  • Maintains balcony usability

Installation Process

Professional bird net installation includes site inspection, stainless steel hook anchoring, corner sealing, and tension alignment to ensure durability and aesthetic finish.

Cost of Bird Netting in Bangalore (2026)

Pricing typically ranges between ₹12 – ₹25 per sq.ft depending on balcony size, height, accessibility, and net material type.
